## 🎯 Frameworks and Standards

### NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology)
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0** - <a href="https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework" target="_blank">Official Site</a>
  - Core Functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
  - **Quick Guide**: <a href="https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf" target="_blank">CSF 2.0 PDF</a>

- **NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5** - Security and Privacy Controls
  - <a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53/rev-5/final" target="_blank">Control Catalog</a>

- **NIST SP 800-61 Rev 2** - Incident Response Guide
  - <a href="https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-61r2.pdf" target="_blank">Incident Handling Guide</a>

- **ISO 31000** - Risk Management Guidelines
